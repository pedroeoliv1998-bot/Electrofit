What ARC does

Six jobs of a great coach, always on.

01 · Wearables

Reads your signals

HRV, sleep, recovery, and strain from your ring and watch — turned into what today's session should be.

In action

Whoop strain 14.2 + 6h sleep → ARC flags amber and swaps the strength block for a recovery flow.

02 · Adaptive Plans

Programs around your life

Builds and re-builds your week against your goals, recovery, and calendar — not a static template.

In action

You skip Tuesday → ARC reflows the week so every target zone still gets hit by Sunday.

03 · Nutrition

Coaches what you eat

Tracks macros and coaches them in context — the smallest change that moves the needle, not generic targets.

In action

Protein short 3 days running → adds a 30g post-session shake, closing the gap without changing meals.

04 · Recovery

Knows when to pull back

The judgment that prevents injury and burnout — when to push, when to rest, dialed into the suit itself.

In action

HRV 38ms, readiness 71% → caps the session at 22 min and lowers the quad channel to 65%.

05 · Mindset

Keeps the habit alive

Proactive and encouraging, never nagging. The nudge at the right moment that turns intention into a streak.

In action

Three days off → "Want a quick 20-minute lower body before your 3pm? You'll feel sharper for it."

06 · Progress

Shows what's working

Tracks strength adaptation across every zone and surfaces the signal — so you can see the suit is working.

In action

Quad output +6% since the deload → ARC nudges volume back up and explains why.
